About "No Bribe – Zambia"

Bribery and corruption are challenges that slow down Zambia’s progress. Every time a bribe is paid, it weakens trust in institutions, denies citizens fair access to services, and discourages honest businesses from growing. Corruption affects everyone — it drains public resources that could build schools, hospitals, and better roads.

What is a Bribe?

A bribe is money, gifts, or favors given to a public official or private worker to receive services that should be free, quicker, or unfairly in your favor. Examples include:

  • Paying an officer to avoid a traffic fine.
  • Giving money at a clinic to be attended to faster.
  • Paying a worker at a government office to skip a queue or get documents approved quickly.
  • Offering gifts to secure a job or contract.

Why Report a Bribe?

By reporting, you help shine a light on corrupt practices. Your reports create data that shows where corruption happens most, helping citizens, civil society, and government institutions to act. Reporting also reminds us that we do not have to accept corruption as normal.

How to Report

Using this platform, you can report a bribe you paid — whether known (you know the official who asked for it) or unknown (an anonymous request for payment). Your name and email are optional, so you may report anonymously if you prefer.

  1. Go to the Report a Bribe page.
  2. Fill in the form with details such as type of bribe, institution, amount (if known), and description.
  3. Submit your report. It will appear in the Reported Cases section.

Together, we can build a Zambia where fairness, honesty, and accountability are the standard.
